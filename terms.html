COMMERCIAL PROTOCOLS

Terms & Conditions of Trade

Commercial trade terms, international Incoterms, payment frameworks, and pre-shipment quality inspection protocols.

1. Commercial Quotations & Proforma Invoices

All quotations, rate indications, and ocean/air freight schedules issued by Indus Roots Exports remain subject to final confirmation upon issuance of a formal, countersigned Proforma Invoice (PI) or Sales Contract. Vessel freight rates are subject to international carrier general rate increases (GRI) and bunker adjustment factors (BAF) unless specifically fixed in a contract.

2. International Trade Incoterms

Transactions are conducted under standard Incoterms 2020 definitions (e.g. FOB Tuticorin / Chennai, CFR, CIF Destination Port, or CIP Air Freight). Risk of loss transfers from seller to buyer in accordance with the agreed Incoterm specified in the commercial contract.

3. Quality Verification & Pre-Shipment Inspection

Products are graded, tested, and inspected against agreed parameters (such as EC, pH, moisture, caliber sizes, packaging specifications) prior to container stuffing. Buyers or their designated third-party inspection agencies (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) are entitled to conduct pre-shipment audits at the stuffing facility at buyer's cost.

4. Biosecurity & Phytosanitary Clearances

Indus Roots Exports procures official Government of India Phytosanitary Certificates and accredited Fumigation Certificates for every agricultural shipment. The buyer remains responsible for obtaining valid import permits and verifying that agreed Indian export treatments meet destination biosecurity criteria.

5. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

Unless otherwise agreed in a bilaterally signed Sales Agreement, commercial contracts are governed by Indian commercial law, with jurisdiction vested in the courts of Coimbatore / Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
