END-TO-END TRADE CAPABILITIES

Import & Export Services

From farm-level origin procurement to deepwater container loading and customs documentation, we deliver a disciplined, frictionless export experience.

WHAT WE DELIVER

Comprehensive Sourcing & Trading Services

We operate as your eyes, ears, and execution partner on the ground in India, eliminating the complexities of overseas trade.

01
🔍

Product Sourcing & Supplier Audit

We identify, vet, and contract verified Tamil Nadu growers and coir processors. We conduct physical facility audits to ensure high hygiene, genuine capacity, and adherence to buyer specifications.

02
🚢

Export Services & Port Execution

Complete management of export clearances from India. We coordinate inland container haulage, customs EDI declarations, factory stuffing, and port loading at Tuticorin (VOC Port) and Chennai.

03
🏷

Private Label & Custom Packaging

Customized packaging solutions including buyer-branded shrink wraps, barcodes, multi-language instructional inserts, branded master cartons, and heat-treated export wooden pallets.

04
🔬

Pre-Shipment Quality Testing

Independent laboratory analysis for electrical conductivity (1:1.5 and 1:5 methods), pH, expansion volume, sand content, moisture, and APEDA-approved pesticide residue MRL tests.

05
📑

Complete Trade Documentation

Accurate, discrepancy-free export paperwork: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (Chamber/AFTA), Phytosanitary Certificate, Fumigation Certificate, and Clean On-Board Ocean Bill of Lading.

06
🌐

Freight & Cold Chain Coordination

Strategic partnerships with leading container shipping lines (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd) for competitive ocean freight, secured equipment allocations, and active reefer temperature monitoring.

DOCUMENTATION ACCURACY

Zero Discrepancy Export Documentation

International customs delays are costly. Indus Roots Exports guarantees meticulous preparation of all regulatory and commercial documents required for seamless clearance at your destination port.

Commercial Invoice Attested with HS Codes & Incoterms
Packing List Gross/Net weights, container/seal nos.
Certificate of Origin Issued by authorized Indian Chamber
Phytosanitary Certificate Government Plant Quarantine verified
Fumigation Certificate Methyl Bromide / Phosphine certified
Bill of Lading / AWB Original 3/3 sets dispatched via courier
PACKAGING EXCELLENCE

Tailored Packaging for Global Distribution

Whether you require bulk industrial packing, greenhouse slab integration, or retail supermarket shelves, we customize packaging to protect product integrity throughout ocean voyages.

Palletized Cocopeat Packing

Blocks stacked uniformly on ISPM-15 heat-treated wooden or fumigated plastic pallets. Secured with heavy-duty corner boards, tight stretch wrap, and nylon strapping.

Approx. 24 Pallets per 40ft HC (24-26 MT)

Ventilated Vegetable Cartons

Heavy-duty 5-ply corrugated CFB boxes engineered with die-cut side ventilation holes to allow air circulation. Lined with food-grade butter paper to prevent condensation damage.

4kg, 5kg & 10kg export master cartons

Breathable Mesh & Jute Sacks

Red Leno mesh bags for small onions and shallots ensuring continuous airflow and skin drying during transit. Traditional multi-wall paper and jute sacks for dried whole spices.

5kg, 10kg, 25kg & 50kg bag formats
