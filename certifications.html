REGULATORY INTEGRITY

Certifications & Accreditations

Complete statutory compliance under the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), APEDA, the Coir Board of India, and Indian Plant Quarantine authorities.

VERIFIABLE STATUTORY CREDENTIALS

Indian Regulatory & Export Registrations

We operate fully within the formal legal frameworks of the Government of India, ensuring every consignment is backed by legitimate trade documentation.

DGFT · GOVT OF INDIA
🏛

Import Export Code (IEC)

Issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry. Certifies Indus Roots Exports as an authorized commercial exporter under Indian customs laws.

Status: Valid & Active for Global Trade
MINISTRY OF COMMERCE
🌾

APEDA Registration (RCMC)

Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority registration, certifying our compliance with international packhouse standards, traceability, and cold chain handling.

Scope: Fresh Fruits & Vegetables
MINISTRY OF MSME
🥥

Coir Board of India

Registration with the statutory Coir Board under the Ministry of MSME, confirming our adherence to Indian standard specifications for washed coco pith, blocks, and horticultural substrate exports.

Scope: Cocopeat & Coir Products
PLANT PROTECTION
🌱

Phytosanitary Certification

Every shipment of agricultural produce and cocopeat undergoes mandatory inspection by Plant Quarantine Officers to certify freedom from quarantine pests, weed seeds, and soil contaminants.

Issuance: Directorate of Plant Protection
INTERNATIONAL MARITIME
🛡

Fumigation & Pest Treatment

Treatments conducted by government-accredited pest control agencies using Methyl Bromide (MBr) or Aluminium Phosphide (ALP), in strict adherence to destination country biosecurity import permits.

Compliance: ISPM-15 & Destination Biosecurity
ORIGIN VERIFICATION
📜

Certificate of Origin (CoO)

Official Certificates of Origin legalized by recognized Indian Chambers of Commerce, enabling overseas buyers to claim preferential customs tariffs under international bilateral trade pacts.

Scope: Non-Preferential & Preferential CoO
