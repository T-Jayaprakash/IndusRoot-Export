ABOUT INDUS ROOTS EXPORTS

A focused start,
built for sustainable growth.

Indus Roots Exports is a Tamil Nadu-based export company connecting international buyers with quality products from India. We started Indus Roots Exports with a clear, uncompromising focus: cocopeat growing media and farm-fresh vegetables sourced directly from Tamil Nadu's established coir and agricultural heartland.

Rather than spreading across twenty unrelated categories from day one, we have chosen to build unshakeable buyer trust, technical consistency, and reliable delivery in the products we know best.

As we expand, our strategic roadmap is clear: scale into proprietary cocopeat processing units, broaden into additional agricultural and natural commodities, and build Indus Roots into your most dependable, full-scale sourcing and export partner from India.

OUR DISCIPLINED COMMITMENT

Source → Verify → Prepare → Export → Deliver

We simplify international sourcing from India by coordinating the entire trade journey with complete transparency and rigorous quality verification.

STAGE 01

Source

Understand buyer technical needs and align with verified Tamil Nadu growers and processors.

STAGE 02

Verify

Sample testing, laboratory verification of EC/pH, and pre-production quality inspection.

STAGE 03

Prepare

Grading, customized packaging, palletizing, labeling, and export documentation readiness.

STAGE 04

Export

Phytosanitary inspection, customs clearance, and containerized loading at Tuticorin or Chennai.

STAGE 05

Deliver

Vessel tracking, original Bill of Lading dispatch, and responsive communication until final arrival.
