Our Story

Indus Roots Exports was born from a simple yet powerful idea

To connect India's roots with the world. Tamil Nadu is one of India's largest coconut-growing regions, and the husk that was once treated as waste has become one of the most valued growing media in modern horticulture.

Alongside coir, the state's farms grow excellent coconuts, garlic, curry leaves, coriander and green chillies. We work closely with local processors and farmers to source, check and pack these products for international buyers, combining traditional know-how with careful, modern handling.

Every enquiry is handled directly by our team, so you know exactly what you are buying, how it is packed and when it will ship.

Mission

To deliver dependable, export-grade coir substrates and fresh Indian produce to growers, distributors and importers worldwide, consistently, transparently and on time.

Vision

To become a trusted name from India for natural growing media and fresh produce, building long-term partnerships across global markets while creating fair value for the farmers and producers we work with.

Values

Quality first, in every batch we ship. Honest specifications and clear pricing. Respect for the farmers and communities behind our products, and for the environment that grows them. A reply to every buyer, every time.
