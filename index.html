Our Story

About Indus Roots Exports

Indus Roots Exports connects Tamil Nadu's farms and processors with buyers around the world. Through Indus Coirs, we supply coir growing media to horticulture growers and distributors. Through Indus Fresh, we export fresh produce, spices and pulses.

Vision

To become a trusted global export and sourcing partner from India.

Mission

To make sourcing from India simple, reliable and professional for international buyers.
