Our Supply Chain Process

A transparent, 7-stage workflow from initial requirement scoping to on-board container loading and final destination delivery.

HOW WE OPERATE

One Seamless Workflow. Zero Friction.

International buyers frequently encounter communication gaps, mismatched specifications, and documentation errors when dealing with fragmented local suppliers.

Indus Roots Exports eliminates these risks by serving as your singular, accountable trade partner in India. Our seven-stage export protocol ensures that every requirement is recorded, verified, inspected, and shipped strictly according to international contract specifications.

STEP-BY-STEP PROGRESSION

The 7 Stages of Indus Roots Trade Execution

Every consignment follows this exact sequence, giving you complete clarity and daily updates.

01
INITIAL CONSULTATION

Requirement & Specification Intake

You share your exact product specifications (grade, electrical conductivity, dimensions, moisture, size calibration), target quantity, delivery incoterms (FOB / CIF), and destination port.

Deliverable: Formal Commercial Quotation & Proforma Invoice within 24 hours.
02
PROCUREMENT

Direct Origin Sourcing

Our on-the-ground procurement team aligns directly with verified Tamil Nadu coir processing yards in Pollachi or harvest growers in Dindigul/Theni to allocate dedicated raw material batches.

Deliverable: Origin lot allocation & pre-production harvest scheduling.
03
QUALITY CONTROL

Rigorous Quality & Laboratory Verification

Raw materials are tested for EC, pH, moisture, sand content, expansion volume, and pesticide MRLs. Any lot deviating from buyer specifications is rejected before packing commences.

Deliverable: Certified laboratory test reports and pre-production photo proof.
04
PACKING & PALLETIZATION

Export Grade Packaging & Labeling

Products are packaged into buyer-specified configurations: palletized shrink-wrapped cocopeat, ventilated corrugated cartons for fresh produce, or custom branded retail sleeves.

Deliverable: High-resolution packing photographs and container loading plan.
05
CUSTOMS COMPLIANCE

Trade Documentation & Phytosanitary Clearances

We draft and verify all regulatory trade paperwork: Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Methyl Bromide Fumigation Certificate, Packing List, and Commercial Invoice.

Deliverable: Advance draft document approval by buyer prior to vessel departure.
06
CONTAINER LOGISTICS

Port Haulage & Ocean / Air Freight Dispatch

Containers are hauled directly to Tuticorin (VOC Port) or Chennai Port, passed through Indian Customs EDI, loaded aboard designated mainline vessels, and sealed with customs RFID seals.

Deliverable: Clean On-Board Bill of Lading (BL) & Container Vessel Tracking Link.
07
COMPLETION & DISCHARGE

Port Discharge & Recurring Partnership

Original negotiable documents are air-couriered (DHL/FedEx) to the buyer or released via telex. We track customs de-vanning and solicit product feedback for recurring seasonal supply contracts.

Deliverable: Completed delivery confirmation & post-arrival satisfaction review.
