Countries We Serve

Connecting Tamil Nadu’s agricultural and coir heartland with commercial buyers, distributors, and greenhouse operators across prime international maritime trade routes.

ESTABLISHED EXPORT CORRIDORS

Strategic Trade Hubs & Ports of Discharge

Our logistics network operates direct vessel slots and air-cargo allocations to major commercial destinations worldwide.

🇦🇪 High Demand Hub

United Arab Emirates

Destination Ports: Jebel Ali (Dubai) / Port Khalid (Sharjah) / DXB Air

Extensive shipments of fresh drumsticks (moringa), indigenous small pink shallots, and green chillies. Transit time: 4-5 days sea freight, same-day air freight.

Key Commodities: Fresh Vegetables & Horticultural Coir
🇸🇦 GCC Partner

Saudi Arabia

Destination Ports: Jeddah Islamic Port / King Abdulaziz Port (Dammam)

Supplying wholesale food markets in Riyadh and Jeddah with small shallots, fresh bananas, and commercial nursery cocopeat blocks for arid soil rejuvenation.

Key Commodities: Shallots, Fresh Bananas, Cocopeat Blocks
🇶🇦 Direct Corridors

Qatar

Destination Ports: Hamad Port (Doha) / DOH Air Cargo

Air-lifted fresh vegetables for Doha supermarket retail chains and palletized cocopeat blocks for Qatar's greening and municipal landscaping initiatives.

Key Commodities: Fresh Drumsticks, Chillies, Coir Substrates
🇴🇲 Fast Transit

Oman

Destination Ports: Sohar Port / Sultan Qaboos Port (Muscat)

Regular containerized reefer shipments of small onions, green chillies, and washed low EC cocopeat for commercial farming projects in the Batinah region.

Key Commodities: Shallots & Low EC Cocopeat
🇸🇬 Southeast Asia Hub

Singapore

Destination Ports: Port of Singapore (PSA) / Changi Air Cargo

Direct feeder transit from Tuticorin and Chennai (5-7 days). Supplies Pasir Panjang wholesale center with fresh moringa, curry leaves, and compact nursery briquettes.

Key Commodities: Fresh Herbs, Drumsticks & Briquettes
🇲🇾 Direct Sea Route

Malaysia

Destination Ports: Port Klang (North & West Port) / Penang

High volume consumer demand for authentic Tamil Nadu shallots and G4 chillies. Commercial coco grow bags for Cameron Highlands greenhouse floriculture.

Key Commodities: Small Onions & Greenhouse Grow Bags
🇬🇧 European Hub

United Kingdom

Destination Ports: Felixstowe / London Gateway / Heathrow Air

Low EC washed cocopeat blocks for commercial horticultural nurseries and retail garden centers across England. Air-freighted exotic Asian fresh produce.

Key Commodities: Low EC Cocopeat & Fresh Moringa
🇳🇱 Horticulture Capital

Netherlands (Rotterdam Gateway)

Destination Ports: Port of Rotterdam / Schiphol (AMS)

Strategic destination for commercial hydroponic coco grow bags and peat moss alternatives serving Westland greenhouse tomato and strawberry growers.

Key Commodities: Greenhouse Slabs & Buffered Coir
🇺🇸 Americas

United States

Destination Ports: New York / New Jersey / Savannah / Los Angeles

High cube container loads of 5kg cocopeat blocks and 650g briquettes for commercial berry growers in California/Florida and nationwide retail packaging distributors.

Key Commodities: 5kg Blocks, Briquettes, Spices
🇿🇦 Southern Africa

South Africa & Mauritius

Destination Ports: Port of Durban / Port Louis (Mauritius)

Direct maritime lines from Tuticorin. Supplying citrus and soft fruit commercial nurseries in Western Cape and vegetable distributors in Port Louis.

Key Commodities: Washed Cocopeat & Export Shallots
