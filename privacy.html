LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. How Indus Roots Exports protects and manages your business inquiry and commercial data.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a commercial quotation, download a product technical specification sheet, or send an inquiry via our website or direct communication channels (WhatsApp, Email), we may collect your name, corporate business name, corporate email address, contact telephone or WhatsApp number, destination country/port, and specific product volume requirements.

2. How We Use Commercial Data

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Formulate accurate proforma invoices and freight estimates from Indian maritime ports.
  • Coordinate sample dispatches and laboratory Certificates of Analysis (COA).
  • Draft international customs documents (Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin).
  • Provide customer service and shipment tracking updates during active consignments.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Indus Roots Exports treats all buyer inquiries, pricing terms, target markets, and shipping specifications with strict professional confidentiality. We never sell, lease, or monetize your commercial data to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Contacting Us Regarding Data Privacy

For inquiries regarding our privacy practices or to request removal of your commercial contact details from our records, please email exports@indusroots.com.
