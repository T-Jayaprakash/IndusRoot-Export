DISCIPLINED VERIFICATION

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Building long-term international buyer trust through measurable physical specifications, certified origin lab tests, and strict pre-shipment inspections.

OUR STANDARDS

Consistent Quality on Every Shipment

At Indus Roots Exports, quality is not a marketing promise — it is a verifiable technical discipline. Every product we export undergoes multi-checkpoint validation before container loading.

In our Indus Coirs division, each raw coir pith lot is sampled across drying yards to verify electrical conductivity (EC) levels, moisture percentages, and expansion yields. In our Indus Fresh division, vegetables undergo rigorous grading for size uniformity, color, and stalk freshness in temperature-controlled packhouses.

Zero Tolerance for Short-Cuts:

If a production batch fails agreed laboratory EC limits or post-harvest grading parameters, it is rejected before stuffing. We believe in buyers who re-order, not one-time transactions.

STEP-BY-STEP VERIFICATION

The 6-Stage Quality Assurance Cycle

Our quality control protocols protect your investment at every juncture from raw harvest to vessel bill of lading.

STAGE 01

Raw Material Origin Vetting

Direct inspection of coconut coir defibering units and agricultural farms in Pollachi and Dindigul. Confirmation of low natural ground water salinity.

STAGE 02

Laboratory Parameter Testing

Conducting 1:1.5 and 1:5 volume extraction tests for Electrical Conductivity (EC), pH testing, sieve analysis (sand < 3%), and moisture analysis (< 15%).

STAGE 03

Packhouse Grading & Sorting

Hand-sorting fresh vegetables for pod straightness, diameter calibration (small shallots 20-35mm), stalk greenness, and zero insect puncture.

STAGE 04

Pre-Cooling & Packing Inspection

Verification of carton tare weights, corrugated box puncture resistance, pallet strapping tension, and pre-cooling to target core temperatures.

STAGE 05

Government Quarantine Clearance

Mandatory inspection by Indian Plant Quarantine Directorate officers, obtaining official Phytosanitary and Fumigation certificates.

STAGE 06

Stuffing & Container Sealing

Container structural check (smell, light leakage, floor dryness), temperature logger activation for reefers, and high-security bolt seal application.

TESTED BENCHMARKS

Certified Quality Parameters

Our standard laboratory test parameters for our two primary product divisions.

Indus Coirs — Laboratory Specifications
Washed Low EC Grade < 0.5 mS/cm (1:1.5 dilution method)
Unwashed High EC Grade > 1.5 mS/cm
pH Range 5.7 to 6.8
Expansion Volume 15 to 16 Litres per kg (75+ Litres / 5kg block)
Moisture Content 10% to 15% Maximum
Sand / Heavy Impurities Less than 3%
Weed Seeds & Pathogens Nil / High heat solar dry cured
Indus Fresh — Produce Quality Standards
Fresh Moringa Pods Length 45-60cm, uniform green, tender non-fibrous seeds
Small Pink Shallots Calibrated 20-30mm / 25-35mm, dry cured papery skin
Green Chillies (G4) 7-10cm, fresh green stalk intact, no yellowing or blemishes
Cavendish Bananas Calibration 39-46mm, finger length 18-22cm, GlobalG.A.P compliant
Pesticide Residue Limits Compliant with EU and GCC MRL statutory regulations
Cold Chain Integrity Continuous temperature logging from packhouse to port
