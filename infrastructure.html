PRODUCTION & LOGISTICS POWERHOUSE

Infrastructure & Facilities

Equipped with dedicated drying yards, automated hydraulic baling presses, temperature-regulated packhouses, and direct deepwater maritime connectivity.

POLLACHI COIR CORRIDOR

Coir Washing Yards & Rotary Screening

Located in the heart of Tamil Nadu's coconut processing capital, our associated yards encompass concrete drying floors and rotary trommel screens to extract raw coco pith from coconut fibers, remove fine sand particles, and naturally sun-cure the raw media.

Washing Water: Treated Sweet Water (< 0.3 EC)
Rotary Sieve: 6mm & 8mm Baby Mesh Sifting
Sun Drying: Direct Tropical Solar Curing
Moisture Control: Monitored to 10% - 15% Max
PROCESSING & COMPRESSION

Automated Hydraulic Baling Units

High-tonnage hydraulic compression presses compress loose, sifted coco pith into dense 5kg blocks at a 5:1 ratio, or into pre-cut grow bag slabs. Computer-controlled weighing mechanisms ensure every block conforms strictly to weight parameters (+/- 300g).

Press Pressure: 150 - 200 Ton Hydraulic Ram
Monthly Capacity: 500+ Metric Tons
UV Bag Extrusion: White/Black Co-extruded 400G
Palletization: Automated Stretch Wrapper
DINDIGUL & THENI BELT

Export Produce Packhouse & Cold Chain Staging

Dedicated post-harvest handling facility designed to APEDA export specifications. Features stainless steel sorting conveyors, mechanical shallot grading tables, food-grade carton assembly, and rapid pre-cooling chambers to lock in farm freshness.

Cold Room Temp: 10°C to 12°C Controlled Humidity
Hygiene Standards: Food-grade Sanitization
Grading Capacity: 15 MT Produce Daily
Packing Lines: CFB Cartons & Leno Mesh Bags
MARITIME GATEWAYS

Container Stuffing & Port Logistics Bay

Direct highway freight corridors connect our facilities to Tuticorin VOC Port (under 3 hours haulage) and Chennai Port. Our stuffing bays feature container docks, electronic weighbridges, and dedicated plug-in points for active refrigerated containers.

Primary Sea Port: Tuticorin VOC Port (~180 km)
Secondary Gateway: Chennai Port & Airport Complex
Container Types: 40ft High Cube & 40ft Reefer
Weighbridge: Government Certified 60 MT
