About Indus Roots Exports

Indus Roots Exports connects Tamil Nadu's farms and processors with buyers around the world. Through Indus Coirs, we supply coir growing media to horticulture growers and distributors. Through Indus Fresh, we export fresh produce, spices and pulses, including garlic, curry leaves, coriander and green chillies.

We work directly with local farmers and processors to source, inspect and pack every consignment to export requirements. Each enquiry is handled personally by our team, so you always know what you are buying, how it is packed and when it will ship.

Vision

To become a trusted global export and sourcing partner from India.

We envision building Indus Roots Exports into a globally recognized Indian export company known for quality, reliability, transparency and long-term business relationships.

Our vision is to represent the strength and diversity of Indian products in international markets while continuously expanding into new product categories and global destinations.

Mission

Our mission is to make sourcing from India simple, reliable and professional for international buyers.

We are committed to:

  • Connecting global buyers with reliable Indian suppliers.
  • Providing products that meet agreed quality and specifications.
  • Building efficient and dependable sourcing networks.
  • Maintaining transparency throughout the business process.
  • Supporting professional export documentation, packaging and logistics coordination.
  • Developing long-term relationships with buyers and suppliers.
  • Continuously identifying new products and opportunities from India for global markets.
Rooted in

Tamil Nadu

Our products come from the coconut groves, coir yards and farms of South India.

The coir belt

Husks are collected from coconut farms and processed into fibre and coco pith at local coir units.

Natural & renewable

Coir is a by-product of coconut farming, a renewable alternative to peat moss that would otherwise go to waste.

Ready for export

Palletised, labelled and documented for shipment through South Indian ports to your destination.
