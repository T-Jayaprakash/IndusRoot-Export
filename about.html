About Indus Roots Exports

A dedicated Indian export house founded on transparency, direct agricultural sourcing, and consistent global delivery.

WHO WE ARE

A focused start,
built to grow.

Indus Roots Exports is a Tamil Nadu-based export company connecting international buyers with quality products from India.

Indus Roots Exports connects India's roots — its soil, its produce, its natural resources — with a direct, reliable path to the world. We started Indus Roots Exports with a clear, disciplined focus: cocopeat growing media and fresh vegetables — sourced directly from Tamil Nadu's established coir and agricultural belt.

Rather than spreading across twenty categories from day one, we have chosen to build genuine quality, batch consistency, and buyer trust in the products we know best. As we grow, our plan is clear: expand into our own cocopeat manufacturing, broaden into additional agricultural and food exports, and build Indus Roots into a dependable, full-scale sourcing and export partner from India.

GEOGRAPHIC ADVANTAGE

Why Tamil Nadu?

Sourced from India's Coir and Agricultural Heartland.

01
🥥

Pollachi Coir Belt

Tamil Nadu's Pollachi and Coimbatore districts form India's most established hub for coconut fiber and coco pith processing, providing fresh, naturally low-salinity husks and sun-drying yards.

02
🥦

Fertile Agri Districts

Surrounded by the Western Ghats and Cauvery delta, districts like Dindigul, Theni, and Trichy yield exceptional small pink shallots, fresh moringa drumsticks, and high-pungency chillies with unmatched flavor.

03
🚢

Strategic Maritime Gateways

Direct highway freight corridors connect our packhouses to Tuticorin Port (V.O. Chidambaranar) and Chennai Port, ensuring swift customs clearance and reduced ocean transit times.
