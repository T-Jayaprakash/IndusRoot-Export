About Us

Connecting India's roots to global markets.

Home/About Us
Indus Roots Exports was born from a simple yet powerful idea

To connect India's roots with the world. Based in Pudukkottai, Tamil Nadu, we export natural coir growing media under our Indus Coirs brand and fresh produce under Indus Fresh.

Tamil Nadu is one of India's largest coconut-growing states. The husk left over after harvest is processed into coco pith, an excellent growing medium now used by greenhouses and nurseries around the world. The same farmland grows outstanding coconuts, garlic, curry leaves, coriander and green chillies.

We work directly with local processors and growers, check every lot against the agreed specification, and pack it for the journey ahead. Our goal is simple: products you can rely on, from a partner who answers.

Mission

To deliver dependable, export-grade coir substrates and fresh Indian produce to growers, distributors and importers worldwide, consistently, transparently and on time.

Vision

To become a trusted name from India for natural growing media and fresh produce, building long-term partnerships across global markets while creating fair value for the farmers and producers we work with.

Values

Quality first, in every batch we ship. Honest specifications and clear pricing. Respect for the farmers and communities behind our products, and for the environment that grows them. A reply to every buyer, every time.
